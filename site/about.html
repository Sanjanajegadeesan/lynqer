Who we are

The Delivery Boy e-Com Service Private Limited is an innovative logistics and technology company transforming India's delivery ecosystem. We provide reliable courier, e-commerce logistics, transportation and digital business solutions for businesses and individuals across urban and rural India.

Our mission is to simplify logistics by combining technology, professional service and a strong franchise network to deliver fast, secure and affordable services nationwide.

Company profile

The Delivery Boy eCom Service Private Limited works as a franchise provider for last-mile delivery operations supporting networks like Flipkart, Delhivery and Shadowfax. We enable local entrepreneurs to run delivery hubs. Franchise partners hire and manage delivery riders for parcel deliveries in their area, and earn commissions based on the deliveries completed under their hub.
